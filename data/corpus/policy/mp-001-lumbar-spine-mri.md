## Scope

This fictional medical policy addresses non-contrast and contrast magnetic resonance
imaging (MRI) of the lumbar spine for members aged 18 and older. Codes in scope
include CPT 72148, 72149, and 72158.

## Coverage criteria

Advanced imaging of the lumbar spine is considered medically necessary when the
member meets criterion A, B, or C.

### Criterion A: conservative therapy failure

All of the following must be documented:

1. At least six weeks of conservative therapy within the preceding six months,
   including at least two of: supervised physical therapy, a documented home exercise
   program, prescription anti-inflammatory or analgesic therapy, or activity
   modification.
2. Persistent or progressive radicular pain or functional limitation after that
   conservative therapy.
3. A clinical examination within the preceding 60 days documenting the current
   neurologic findings.

### Criterion B: red flag findings

Conservative therapy is not required when any of the following red flags is
documented. These cases are expedited:

- New or progressive motor deficit
- Suspected cauda equina syndrome, including saddle anesthesia or new bowel or
  bladder dysfunction
- Suspected spinal infection, including fever with new back pain, intravenous drug
  use, or recent spinal procedure
- Known or suspected malignancy with new back pain
- Significant trauma, or minor trauma in a member with known osteoporosis

### Criterion C: surgical or interventional planning

Imaging is approved when a surgical or interventional pain consultation has occurred
within the preceding 90 days and the treating specialist has documented that the
imaging will determine the operative plan.

## Documentation required with the request

- Office notes covering the conservative therapy trial, with dates
- Current neurologic examination findings
- The specific clinical question the imaging is expected to answer

## When the policy does not apply

This policy does not apply to imaging performed during an inpatient admission or in
an emergency department, which follow the facility authorization process instead.

## Turnaround time

Standard determinations are issued within 14 calendar days of receiving the request.
Expedited determinations, including all Criterion B red flag cases, are issued within
72 hours.
