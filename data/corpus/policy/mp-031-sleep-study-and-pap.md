## Scope

Attended in-laboratory polysomnography, home sleep apnea testing, and positive airway
pressure (PAP) devices and supplies.

## Site of service

Home sleep apnea testing is the preferred initial test for adults with suspected
uncomplicated obstructive sleep apnea. Attended in-laboratory polysomnography is
approved when any of the following is documented:

- Suspected central sleep apnea or a sleep-related hypoventilation disorder
- Significant cardiopulmonary comorbidity, including moderate to severe heart failure
  or chronic obstructive pulmonary disease requiring oxygen
- Neuromuscular disease affecting respiration
- A prior home sleep apnea test that was technically inadequate or negative despite a
  high clinical suspicion
- Suspected narcolepsy or a parasomnia requiring attended monitoring

## PAP device initial coverage

A PAP device is medically necessary when both of the following are documented:

1. A face-to-face clinical evaluation documenting symptoms and findings consistent
   with obstructive sleep apnea.
2. A qualifying sleep test with an apnea-hypopnea index of 15 or more events per
   hour, or 5 to 14 events per hour with documented excessive daytime sleepiness,
   hypertension, ischemic heart disease, or a history of stroke.

## PAP continued coverage

Continued coverage after the first 90 days requires documented adherence: usage on at
least 70% of nights over a consecutive 30-day period within the first 90 days, with
usage of at least four hours per night. A clinical re-evaluation between days 31 and
91 must document symptom improvement.

Members who do not meet the adherence requirement may requalify after a repeat
face-to-face evaluation and a documented plan to address the barrier to use.

## Supplies

Replacement supply quantities follow the published supply schedule. Requests above
the schedule require documentation of the clinical reason.
