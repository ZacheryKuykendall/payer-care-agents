## Scope

Therapeutic continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) systems and related supplies for
members with diabetes mellitus. Representative codes: HCPCS A4239, E2103.

## Coverage criteria

A therapeutic CGM is considered medically necessary when all of the following are
documented:

1. A diagnosis of type 1 diabetes mellitus, or type 2 diabetes mellitus treated with
   insulin, or a documented history of problematic hypoglycemia.
2. The member or their caregiver has been trained on the device and is able to use
   the data to adjust therapy.
3. An in-person or telehealth visit with the treating prescriber within the preceding
   six months, and a plan for follow-up at least every six months.
4. For members on insulin, a documented insulin regimen including the number of daily
   administrations or an insulin pump.

## Problematic hypoglycemia

Problematic hypoglycemia means either of the following within the preceding
12 months:

- A level 2 hypoglycemic event below 54 mg/dL recurring despite a documented
  adjustment of the treatment plan
- A level 3 hypoglycemic event requiring the assistance of another person

## Continuation of therapy

Continued coverage requires documentation every 12 months that the member is using
the device and attending the required follow-up visits. Requests for continuation do
not require a repeat of the initial training documentation.

## Not covered

Non-therapeutic or professional-use-only continuous glucose monitors used solely for
intermittent diagnostic assessment are not covered under this fictional policy when
billed in addition to a therapeutic device in the same period.
