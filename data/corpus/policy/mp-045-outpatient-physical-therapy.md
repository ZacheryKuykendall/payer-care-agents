## Initial authorization

The first six visits of outpatient physical or occupational therapy in a new episode
of care do not require prior authorization. An episode of care is a new condition, a
new injury, or a documented significant change in an existing condition.

## Continued authorization

Visits beyond the initial six require authorization supported by:

1. A current plan of care signed by the treating therapist and the referring
   provider.
2. Objective, measurable functional goals with baseline and current values, such as
   range of motion in degrees, a standardized outcome measure score, or documented
   gait distance.
3. Evidence of measurable progress toward those goals, or a documented clinical
   rationale for continuing without measurable progress.

## Maintenance therapy

Therapy whose purpose is to maintain current function rather than to improve it is
covered when the skills of a licensed therapist are required to carry it out safely
and effectively. Lack of improvement alone is not a basis for denial.

## Visit limits

Visit limits are defined in each plan's benefit summary, not in this policy. A
clinical denial under this policy and a benefit exhaustion under the plan's visit
limit are different outcomes and generate different member letters.
