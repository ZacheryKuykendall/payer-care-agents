## Plan overview

Northwind Advantage Choice (PPO) is a fictional Medicare Advantage PPO. Members may
use out-of-network providers who accept Medicare at a higher cost share. No referral
is required to see a specialist.

- Monthly plan premium: $29
- Annual medical deductible: $0 in-network, $500 out-of-network
- Maximum out-of-pocket, in-network: $6,700
- Maximum out-of-pocket, combined in- and out-of-network: $10,000

## Primary care and specialist visits

| Service | In-network | Out-of-network |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary care provider office visit | $5 copay | 30% coinsurance |
| Specialist office visit | $45 copay | 40% coinsurance |
| Annual wellness visit | $0 copay | 30% coinsurance |
| Telehealth visit | $0 copay | not covered |
| Urgent care center | $50 copay | $50 copay |
| Emergency room | $120 copay | $120 copay |

No referral is required for specialist care under this PPO plan. Prior authorization
requirements still apply regardless of network status.

## Out-of-network rules

Out-of-network providers must accept Medicare assignment. Members are responsible for
the out-of-network cost share plus any balance billing permitted under the provider's
contract status. Out-of-network services that require prior authorization and do not
receive it may be denied as a plan administrative denial.
