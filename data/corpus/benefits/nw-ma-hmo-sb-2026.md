## Plan overview

Northwind Advantage Gold (HMO) is a fictional Medicare Advantage plan with Part D
prescription drug coverage. Members choose a primary care provider (PCP) from the
Northwind HMO network and receive care from network providers except for emergency
care, urgently needed services, and out-of-area renal dialysis.

- Monthly plan premium: $0
- Annual medical deductible: $0
- Maximum out-of-pocket (MOOP), in-network: $4,900 per year
- Service area: fictional counties used for demonstration only

## Primary care and specialist visits

| Service | Member cost share (in-network) |
| --- | --- |
| Primary care provider (PCP) office visit | $0 copay |
| Specialist office visit | $35 copay |
| Annual wellness visit | $0 copay |
| Telehealth visit with a network provider | $0 copay |
| Urgent care center | $45 copay |
| Emergency room | $110 copay, waived if admitted within 24 hours |

A referral from the member's PCP is required before seeing most network specialists.
Referrals are not required for the following: annual routine women's health exams,
in-network behavioral health outpatient visits, flu and pneumonia vaccinations, and
routine vision or hearing exams covered under the supplemental benefits below.

Out-of-network specialist care is not covered under this HMO plan except for
emergency care, urgently needed services, and out-of-area dialysis.

Advanced imaging, outpatient surgery, and skilled nursing facility admissions require
prior authorization. See the Northwind 2026 Prior Authorization List.

## Part D prescription drug cost share

Cost share applies after any applicable deductible. The 2026 Part D deductible for
this fictional plan is $0 for Tiers 1 and 2 and $150 for Tiers 3 through 5.

| Tier | 30-day retail supply | 90-day mail order supply |

| Tier 2 - Generic | $10 | $20 |

## What is not covered

This fictional plan does not cover cosmetic surgery, custodial care, services outside
the United States except limited worldwide emergency coverage, or services from
providers excluded from federal health care programs.
