## Plan overview

Northwind Standard PPO is a fictional employer-sponsored preferred provider
organization plan.

- Annual deductible: $1,500 individual / $3,000 family (in-network)
- Coinsurance after deductible: 20% in-network, 40% out-of-network
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $6,000 individual / $12,000 family (in-network)
- Embedded individual deductible applies within family coverage

## Office and outpatient cost share

| Service | In-network member cost |
| --- | --- |
| Primary care office visit | $30 copay, deductible waived |
| Specialist office visit | $60 copay, deductible waived |
| Virtual urgent care | $15 copay, deductible waived |
| Preventive care (USPSTF grade A and B) | $0, deductible waived |
| Advanced imaging (MRI, CT, PET) | 20% coinsurance after deductible |
| Outpatient physical therapy | $40 copay, 30 visit annual limit |
| Behavioral health outpatient | $30 copay, deductible waived |

## Pharmacy cost share

| Tier | 30-day retail |
| --- | --- |
| Tier 1 - Generic | $12 |
| Tier 2 - Preferred brand | $45 |
| Tier 3 - Non-preferred brand | $90 |
| Tier 4 - Specialty | 25% coinsurance, $350 maximum per fill |

Specialty drugs must be filled through the Northwind specialty pharmacy network after
the first fill.

## Coordination with a health savings account

This fictional plan is not a qualified high-deductible health plan and is not
compatible with a health savings account. Members may use a general-purpose flexible
spending account.
